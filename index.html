[ 07 — Governance & security ]

Answerable by design.

We expect to be asked where data lives, who can reach it, and what happens when someone withdraws consent. These are the standing answers.

Infrastructure

Single Australian region

All production systems run in one Australian cloud region. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using managed keys. Access is least-privilege and logged.

Retention

Held only while needed

Data is retained for the period a customer's consent covers, and deleted when that consent ends or is withdrawn. Withdrawal is available at any time, without explanation.

Separation

Tenancy enforced in the model

Access boundaries are enforced at the data layer rather than the interface. A request outside a caller's boundary is refused, not filtered.

Disclosure

A named security contact

Vulnerability reports reach a monitored address and are acknowledged. Researchers acting in good faith will not be pursued. Report an issue.

● Application & data

Encryption

TLS in transit, encrypted at rest

Traffic is served over modern TLS only. Data at rest is encrypted with managed keys held in a cloud key service, never in application code or configuration.

Secrets

No credentials in the codebase

API keys, tokens and database credentials live in a managed secret store with rotation. Nothing sensitive is committed to source control.

Edge

Attack filtering at the perimeter

A managed web application firewall and DDoS mitigation sit in front of the application, with rules updated by the provider as new threats appear.

● Infrastructure & code

Infrastructure as code

Environments are reproducible

Infrastructure is declared in code and applied through review, so changes are peer-checked, auditable, and recoverable after an outage.

Dependencies

Third-party libraries are scanned

Automated dependency scanning runs against the codebase, and known-vulnerable packages block a release rather than being noted after it.

Release

Reviewed before it ships

Code is peer reviewed and tested in a separate environment before production. Static analysis runs on every change.

● Assurance & people

Independent assurance

SOC 2 Type 2 — on the roadmap

We do not hold a SOC 2 report today and will not imply otherwise. We can share our current control documentation, and our target date, on request.

Testing

Third-party penetration testing

We intend to engage a certified assessor ahead of general release, and to repeat testing on major changes.

People

Access ends when the role does

Production access is granted by role, reviewed on a set cycle, and revoked on departure. Devices used for production work are managed and encrypted.

We publish the difference rather than blur it.

Responsible disclosure

Found something? Tell us, and we won't come after you.

Reports go to a monitored address and are acknowledged. Researchers acting in good faith — no data exfiltration, no service disruption, no third-party impact — will not be pursued.

Report an issue → Acknowledged within 3 business days

    What we collect

    Where you use RateCue through a licensed mortgage broker, we may hold information about your home loan and the property securing it — loan balance, interest rate, product features, lender, repayment history, offset position and property valuation estimates.

    How we obtain it

    Loan and account information reaches us either from you, from your broker on your instruction, or through an accredited data access arrangement that you authorise. We do not obtain your banking credentials and we do not initiate transactions on your accounts.

    Information is held in Australian data centre regions operated by our cloud provider. Access is limited to personnel who need it to operate the service, and access is logged.

    We disclose information to your broker, because that is the purpose of the service. We do not sell personal information and we do not disclose it for marketing by third parties.

    We may disclose information where required by law, or to service providers who help us operate the platform under contractual confidentiality obligations.

    Consent and withdrawal

    You can withdraw consent for ongoing monitoring at any time by contacting us or your broker. On withdrawal we stop collecting new information about your loan.

    You can ask us to delete information we hold, subject to any period we are legally required to retain records.
